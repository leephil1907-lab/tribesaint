The House

Tribe Culture was established MMXXVI. One idea, two marks — and twelve pieces to begin.

The premise

We are not just a brand.
We are a movement.

Tribe is where culture, creativity and community collide. We make heavy garments for people who would rather discover something true than repeat something comfortable.

Everything starts from the quote printed across the back of the Saint tee: the greatest pleasure is to discover new truth — the next is to shake off old prejudices. The clothes are the argument, not the decoration.

The code

Four lines we hold to

01 · Community

Nobody wears the crest alone

We create space for people to belong, collaborate and grow. The list, the drop, the room — it is all one room.

02 · Creativity

Ideas become objects

Print studies, film, garment construction. We would rather make one thing properly than five things loudly.

03 · Expression

The cut is the same. The meaning is yours

Individuality, style, sound, art and identity. We build the blank; you decide what it says.

04 · Impact

Built with intention

Small runs, real stock counts, honest delivery promises. We create opportunities for our people, city by city.

The marks

Two marks carry the house

The Saint print

Arched collegiate lettering with three doves breaking the baseline, the doctrine line beneath, and TRIBE · twin globes · SOCIETY at the hem. Blood red on black, or red on white.

The TC monogram

An interlocking monogram cut from the baroque crest. It sits at the left chest, on the cap crown, and on every seal of the house.
